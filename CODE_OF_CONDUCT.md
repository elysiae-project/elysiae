# Elysiae Project Code of Conduct

The Elysiae project values a civil community, welcome to opinions from new and old users alike. Please read through this code of conduct to understand what we expect from the community

## 1. General Rules

We intend for the Elysiae community to be safe and welcoming. As such, we do not want to see any form of harassment of bullying on anything we control. Examples of this are:

- Trolling
- Doxxing
- Any form of Racial/Sexual/Gender-based discrimination
- Any form of harassment of members of the Elysiae project, weather it is on official channels or not

There is no set punishment for violating these rules; punishments for a violation is up to a moderator or higher member of the Elysiae Project

## 2. AI Usage Policy

The Elysiae Project allows the use of AI assisted development tools. The contributor agrees to be personally accountable for every change they propose to the project, regardless of the tools they use.

### General Principles

By contributing to Elysiae, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

- You understand every change you submit to the project, including its expected behavior and implementation details.
- You understand your designs and implementation well enough to answer questions about them during review.
- You review every change you submit, including AI generated code.
- You take responsibility for the accuracy and quality of your contributions.

You acknowledge that you are personally accountable for every change you submit to the project, regardless of how it was generated.

### Acceptable Use

Some examples of acceptable use of AI assisted development tools include:

- Implementation of specified features, based on detailed specifications from the contributor
- Assisting in debugging or investigating issues
- Refactoring, optimization, or alternate implementation suggestions
- Explanation of unfamiliar code, APIs, or compiler errors
- Resolution of merge conflicts, when the desired outcome is specified by the contributor
- General purpose boilerplate generation, followed by review

### Unacceptable Use

The following are examples of unacceptable use of AI assisted development tools:

- Open ended architectural or implementation questions (see acceptable use above)
- Submitting code that you did not review
- Submitting code that you cannot explain or defend
- Using an AI to replace your judgment when designing or reviewing code
- Blindly copying suggestions, fixes, or refactors proposed by an AI

A contribution may be considered to have been generated by _vibe coding_, even if it was created using different tools than those described above.

### Enforcement

Contributions that fail to meet these requirements may be rejected.

A warning will be issued for the first offense. Repeat offenders may be blocked from contributing to the project.

## 3. Public Knowledge of content created by The Elysiae Project

Elysiae is one of many "Anime Game" launchers available for download by Linux users. Many popular alternatives to Elysiae prefer their users to not mention the names of the project on the internet, in fears that their project could get struck down by the "Anime Game Company". The Elysiae project believes that these fears are largely unfounded, so long as developers are not making money off their projects and aren't actively breaking more severe rules layed out in the terms of service of each game (i.e. providing cheats/plugins).

The Elysiae Launcher only acts as a wrapper for our game downloader, irmin (which in turn gets download URLs from data that is available to the public) and for phlogiston, our custom proton runtime that launches "Anime Games". Elysiae does not and will not add the ability to add cheats/plugins to games, and will not be accepting donations for this project. 

As such, we believe that it is okay for you to mention this project to others you know online and offline. No harm should come from this. You may use Elysiae's full name when mentioning this project
